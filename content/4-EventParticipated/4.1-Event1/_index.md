### 1. Purpose of the Event

The Kickoff FCAJ Buildrathon 2026 – Season 01 officially launched the new season, marking the beginning of a 6-month journey of learning, hands-on practice, and tackling AI and Cloud challenges.

Beyond introducing the competition, the program gave participants access to real-world experience in building and deploying AI Agents in enterprise environments through the talkshow **"The Thinking Behind Building The Bot"**, co-organized by CMC Global and VietAWS.

The event also offered opportunities to connect directly with industry experts, analyze technical challenges, and explore career development through 1-on-1 consultation sessions with CMC Global's Recruitment team.

### 3. Key Highlights

**AI Agent Thinking in Practice & Enterprise Perspective**

One of the most noteworthy parts of the program was the discussion on how to build and deploy AI Agents to solve real business problems.

- **Architecture-first approach:** Before writing any code, define the system structure and data flow clearly to make the development process smoother and more scalable.
- **Enterprise-driven mindset:** Building an AI Agent is not just about model capability — performance, security, and operational cost all need equal attention.

**Breaking Down Challenges with Experts**

The technical challenge analysis session offered practical perspectives on handling common engineering problems.

- **Test analysis:** Learning how to break down requirements, identify common mistakes, and choose the right solution approach.
- **Real-world experience sharing:** Experts shared lessons from actual project deployments, covering both what worked and what did not.

**Career Fast-Track & Community Spirit**

- **1-on-1 with CMC Global HR:** Direct conversations with the recruitment team to understand job requirements and receive personal feedback on skills and direction.
- **Pay It Forward spirit:** The collaboration between VietAWS and CMC Global created a knowledge-sharing environment that connects people who share a passion for technology.

---

### 4. What I Learned

**Design Thinking**

- **Architecture-first approach:** Understood the importance of defining system architecture and processing flow before starting feature development on any Bot.
- **Solve the real problem first:** An AI product does not need to use the latest technology — it needs to solve a specific problem and meet actual user needs.

**Technical Architecture**

- **Breaking down complex requirements:** With complex use cases, the ability to decompose them into smaller, manageable parts makes implementation, testing, and adjustment much easier.
- **Improving stability:** Recognized the need to focus on error handling, edge cases, and response time optimization to build a more reliable AI Bot.

**Personal Development Strategy**

- **Understanding hiring standards:** Insights from CMC Global HR provided a clearer picture of the skills and knowledge needed when applying for technology-related roles.
- **Building a practical portfolio:** Participating in real projects and competitions is the most effective way to accumulate experience and add concrete work to a personal capability profile.

---

### 5. Application to Work

- **Restructure the Bot-building process:** Focus on designing architecture and defining the processing flow before building individual features, rather than starting with isolated functions.
- **Prepare for FCAJ Buildrathon 2026:** Apply problem decomposition techniques to prepare better for competition challenges and improve team collaboration.
- **Optimize personal productivity:** Explore AI Agent tools that can automate repetitive daily tasks to save time and increase work efficiency.

### 6. Event Experience

The atmosphere at the Kickoff FCAJ Buildrathon 2026 (Season 01) combined with the CMC Global and VietAWS talkshow was lively, with a large turnout of people interested in AI and Cloud.

**Learning from Expert Speakers**

Sharing sessions from **Truong Bui (Tech Lead, CMC Global)**, **Thien Lu**, and **Phong Pham** offered different perspectives — from technical depth to project organization and real-world deployment. The content was clear and focused on problems commonly encountered in practice.

**Hands-on Technical Experience**

The real test analysis section was the most memorable part. Watching experts break down problems and propose solutions made clear that technical knowledge alone is not enough — having the right thinking approach and problem-solving mindset matters just as much.

**Networking and Exchange**

The 1-on-1 career consulting with CMC Global's Recruitment team was genuinely valuable. The conversations provided insight into real working environments and what skills companies are actively looking for in candidates. The spirit of sharing and mutual support from the VietAWS and CMC Global community was one of the most meaningful aspects of the event.

---

### 7. Key Takeaways

- **Architecture is the foundation:** A well-thought-out system structure makes development, maintenance, and future scaling significantly easier. Starting with architecture before code is not overhead — it is the foundation.
- **Practice deepens understanding:** Theory becomes meaningful only when applied to real problems. Doing it yourself reveals gaps that reading alone cannot surface.
- **Proactive networking accelerates growth:** Talking with experts, peers, and recruiters broadens perspective, provides concrete career direction, and opens doors that passive learning cannot.
